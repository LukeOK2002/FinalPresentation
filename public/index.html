What are transposable elements

Transposable Elements (TEs) are genetic sequences capable of copying themselves and spreading throughout the genome

Either by creating an RNA intermediate (retrotransposition)...

Or by excising itself (transposition)

How often does chance alone give a TSD signal?

Each round draws two random 6-bp flanks. Any TA dinucleotide lights up yellow; TA in both flanks = a "TSD signal" produced by chance. Everyone's rounds are pooled, and the yellow line should settle near the 9.66% background rate, inside the shaded chance band. Keys: R = run a round, A = auto-run (fallback if phones can't connect). Model: bases are drawn with A = T ≈ 26.0%, C = G ≈ 24.0%, which is what makes P(TA in both 6-bp flanks) exactly 9.66% (uniform bases would give 8.37%).
